## The Three Multipliers in Datadog's Pricing

Datadog does not have one price; it has **four independent meters** that compound together:

1. **Infrastructure Hosts** (\$15–\$23/host/mo)
2. **APM Hosts** (\$31–\$40/host/mo)
3. **Log Ingestion + Indexing** (\$0.10/GB ingested + \$1.70 per million indexed events for 15-day retention)
4. **Custom Metrics** (\$5 per 100 metrics/month — where a single `user_id` or `pod_name` tag explosion can add \$800/mo overnight)

When we modeled a realistic 20-host microservice cluster generating **500 GB/month of logs** (~16.6 GB/day) and **4,000 custom metrics**, Datadog's monthly bill hit **\$2,640/mo**.

## Cost Simulation: Datadog vs SigNoz Self-Hosted

SigNoz stores logs, traces, and metrics inside **ClickHouse** using OpenTelemetry (`otel-collector`). Because ClickHouse compresses structured logs at 6×–10× ratios using ZSTD, 500 GB of raw JSON logs occupies roughly **65 GB on disk** for a 15-day retention window.

## Sizing ClickHouse So It Doesn't OOM

ClickHouse is fast, but its default memory settings assume it owns the entire machine. If you deploy SigNoz on a shared VPS without capping ClickHouse's cache, the Linux OOM killer will terminate it during heavy log queries.

In our blueprint's `docker-compose.yml`, we enforce three guardrails:

- **Dedicated NVMe storage**: Minimum 300 GB NVMe for 500 GB/month ingestion with 30-day TTL.
- **Explicit ClickHouse memory cap**: `max_server_memory_usage_to_ram_ratio = 0.65` on a 32 GB RAM node.
- **OTel Collector batching & tail-sampling**: Drop health-check `/healthz` spans at the collector before they ever hit ClickHouse.

```yaml
services:
  otel-collector:
    image: signoz/signoz-otel-collector:0.102.9
    command:
      - "--config=/etc/otel-collector-config.yaml"
    deploy:
      resources:
        limits:
          memory: 2G
```

## The Verdict

If your team is under 5 engineers and needs zero-maintenance dashboards today, **SigNoz Cloud** or a tightly capped Grafana Cloud tier is a great middle ground. Once you cross **500 GB/month in logs** or **15+ hosts**, running SigNoz on a dedicated 32 GB NVMe box saves roughly **\$29,500/year** while giving you full OpenTelemetry ownership.
